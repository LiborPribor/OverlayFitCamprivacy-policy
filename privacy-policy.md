## 1. Introduction
OverlayFit – Fitness Camera (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Device Information:** Model, operating system, and basic technical details for app performance.  
- **Camera Access:** The App uses your device’s camera to capture photos and videos. These files remain on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.  
- **Storage Access:** Required to save and manage photos/videos locally.  
- **Advertising Data:** If you use the free version, third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob) may collect anonymous identifiers for ad personalization.  
- **In-App Purchases:** If you purchase premium features (e.g., ad-free version), Google Play may process payment information. We do not store your payment details.

---

## 3. How We Use Information
- To provide core functionality (camera overlays, fitness tracking).  
- To improve app performance and user experience.  
- To display ads (in free version).  
- To enable premium features when purchased.  

---

## 4. Data Sharing
- We do **not** sell or share personal data with third parties.  
- Ads and analytics may be provided by trusted third-party services (Google AdMob, Google Play Services).  
- Payment processing is handled securely by Google Play.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy
- The App is **not directed to children under 13 years of age**.  
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.  
- If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

---

## 6. User Rights
- You may uninstall the App at any time, which stops all data collection.  
- You may disable ad personalization in your Google account settings.  
- You may contact us to request clarification or deletion of any data we may hold indirectly (through third-party services).

---

## 7. Security
- We take reasonable measures to protect your information.  
- No data is stored on external servers by us; all photos/videos remain on your device.
